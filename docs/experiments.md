# ORBITALIS — Astrophysical Presets & Experiments Guide

## 1. Solar System (3D)
* **Bodies:** Sun + Mercury, Venus, Earth with nested Moon sub-orbit, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with 3D rings, Uranus, Neptune.
* **Physics Check:** Verify Kepler's Third Law ($T^2 \propto a^3$) and watch the Earth-Moon system maintain a stable sub-orbit around the Sun.

## 2. Binary Stars & Circumbinary Planet
* **Bodies:** Alpha Centauri A & B in mutual orbit around their common barycenter.
* **Physics Check:** Enable "Barycenter" to observe both stars orbit the yellow crosshair marker while Tatooine orbits outside the binary pair.

## 3. Figure-8 Three-Body Choreography
* **Discovery:** Chenciner & Montgomery (2000).
* **Physics Check:** Three stars of equal mass chase each other perpetually on an exact figure-8 track without collision.

## 4. Asteroid Belt & Gas Giant Resonance
* **Bodies:** 180 asteroid planetesimals between Mars and Jupiter.
* **Physics Check:** Observe how Jupiter's periodic gravitational perturbations sculpt the asteroid belt and clear orbital resonance zones (Kirkwood gaps).

## 5. Gravitational Slingshot (Voyager Hyperbolic Flyby)
* **Bodies:** Sun, Jupiter, and a lightweight interstellar probe.
* **Physics Check:** The probe approaches Jupiter on a hyperbolic trajectory. Observe its speed jump as it steals orbital momentum from Jupiter and accelerates out of the solar system.

## 6. 100–500 Star Cluster Benchmark
* **Physics Check:** Benchmark the direct $O(N^2)$ N-body gravity calculation at 200 to 500 interacting bodies with 60 FPS rendering.
